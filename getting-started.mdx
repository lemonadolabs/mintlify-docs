Lemonado helps you ask questions about your marketing and turn the answers into reports. Start with one company, one connection, and one question.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and add your company">
    [Open Lemonado](https://app.lemonado.io) and follow the setup prompts. Add your company name and website so Lemonado can learn about the business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect one source">
    Open **Connectors**, choose the tool you use most, and follow the sign-in steps. Select the account you want to work with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the context">
    Click the gear beside the company name. Review **Memory** and **Brand**, and correct anything that is missing or out of date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a useful result">
    Open **New chat** and try: “Summarize last week's ads. What changed, and what should I look at first?”
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Connectors bring the information Lemonado needs into one workspace.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/connectors.png" alt="Search for your marketing tools in Lemonado's Connectors page" />
</Frame>

Check the dates and sources in the answer. Ask a follow-up, or ask Lemonado to turn it into a report.

<Tip>
  Working with clients? Use an [agency workspace](/agencies) to keep each client's data and work separate.
</Tip>
