Lemonado is an AI marketing teammate that works with the tools and company context you already have. It helps marketing teams get clear answers and agencies keep client work separate.

You only need one connected source and one clear question to begin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your workspace">
    Pick **Team** if you work on one company. Pick **Agency** if you manage clients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your company or first client">
    Enter the company name and website. Lemonado uses them to prepare useful background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect one source">
    Open **Connectors**, choose a tool, and follow the sign-in steps. Start with the source you use most.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the context">
    Open the gear icon. Check **Memory** and **Brand** so Lemonado understands the company and its voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a useful result">
    Return to **Home** and type a clear request. For example: “Summarize last month’s paid media results and tell me what needs attention.”
  </Step>
</Steps>
