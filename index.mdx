Lemonado is an AI-native marketing data platform that unifies your ad platforms, analytics tools, and revenue data into a single queryable layer. From there, your team can chat with live data, build dashboards and reports in Studio, monitor performance with proactive agents, and connect live marketing data directly to Claude, ChatGPT, and other AI tools via MCP.

## Get started in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [data.lemonado.io](https://data.lemonado.io) — no credit card required. Your 10-day free trial starts immediately on the AI-First plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a data source">
    Go to **Data** in the sidebar and connect your first integration — Google Ads, Meta Ads, or Google Analytics are great starting points. Connections take about 30 seconds via OAuth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Open **Chat** and ask a question in plain language: *"What was my ROAS last week by campaign?"* Lemonado runs a live SQL query against your connected data and returns an answer with the numbers to back it up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a report or connect to AI">
    Ask Lemonado to build a report in Studio, set up a proactive agent to monitor performance, or connect your workspace to Claude or ChatGPT via MCP.
  </Step>
</Steps>
