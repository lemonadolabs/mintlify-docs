Lemonado pricing is built around credits — consumed when AI does work for you. Seats, ad spend, and connected data are unlimited on all plans. Choose a plan based on how much AI work you need and which features matter most.

## What are credits?

Credits are used whenever the AI does work — simpler queries use fewer credits, complex cross-platform analysis uses more. Every question you ask, report you generate, or agent run you trigger consumes credits proportional to the work done.

| Query | Work done | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| How much did we spend on Meta Ads yesterday? | Single data point lookup | 1 |
| Why are Google Ads conversions down this week? | Week-over-week comparison, driver analysis | 7 |
| Build a branded Q2 performance Studio | Multi-source analysis, KPI calculation, full Studio generation | 30 |

You can top up credits at any time at **$50 per 500 credits**, or upgrade your plan to increase your monthly allocation.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    AI features pause until your credits reset at the start of your next billing cycle. You can top up at **$50 per 500 credits** or upgrade your plan at any time to get more credits immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the current billing period. There is no lock-in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is annual billing available?">
    Yes. Annual plans come with a discount and are billed once a year. Contact [hello@lemonado.io](mailto:hello@lemonado.io) to switch to annual billing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add more credits without upgrading?">
    Yes. Top up credits at **$50 per 500 additional credits** on any plan. Top-ups take effect immediately and carry you through until your next billing reset.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
