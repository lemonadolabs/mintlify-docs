The Prompt Library is a collection of pre-built prompts created by the Lemonado team and community for common marketing analysis tasks. Use them directly in Lemonado Chat or run them through a connected MCP tool like Claude or ChatGPT — your live data powers every answer.

## Google Ads prompts

1. **Google Ads Essentials: Prompt Starter 10-Pack** — Ten starter prompts covering spend analysis, ROAS, conversion monitoring, and keyword performance. Great for teams new to querying Google Ads data.
2. **Brand vs. Non-Brand Ad Budget Optimization** — Compares performance and spend efficiency between branded and non-branded keyword campaigns.
3. **Google Ads Conversion Health Monitoring** — Flags conversion tracking gaps, missing tags, and underperforming conversion events across all campaigns.
4. **Identify Wasted Spend on Branded Keywords** — Surfaces branded keywords where you're spending on clicks you'd likely have captured organically.

## Meta Ads prompts

1. **Daily Meta Ads Spend Check** — Quick daily check across all active campaigns: total spend, pacing vs. budget, and any campaigns that stopped delivering.
2. **Flag Meta Ads Zero Spend & Overspend Risks** — Identifies ad sets with zero spend in the last 24 hours and ad sets pacing over daily budget.
3. **Diagnose Ad Set Spend Gaps** — Finds Meta ad sets that have paused or underspent relative to their allocation, with likely causes.

## Cross-platform prompts

1. **Detect Wasted Ad Spend Across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn** — Runs across all three platforms simultaneously to find high-spend, low-conversion segments.
2. **Improve LinkedIn Ads Creative Performance** — Evaluates creative-level performance data on LinkedIn and surfaces recommendations for underperforming ads.
3. **Identify Warm Prospects from LinkedIn Ads** — Finds LinkedIn audiences with above-average engagement but below-average conversion — warm leads that haven't converted yet.

## Google Analytics prompts

1. **Daily Website Traffic Source Ranking** — Ranks traffic sources by sessions, new users, and conversion rate for the current day.
2. **Device Conversion Performance Analysis** — Compares mobile vs. desktop performance across conversions, bounce rate, and session duration.

## Revenue & Commerce prompts

1. **Evaluate Stripe Customer Value and Retention** — Segments customers by lifetime value, subscription status, and churn risk.
2. **Find Revenue Lost at Checkout** — Identifies drop-off points in the Stripe checkout flow and estimates revenue lost.
3. **Analyze Marketplace Revenue by Marketing Channel** — *(For Sharetribe + Stripe users)* Attributes marketplace revenue to the marketing channels that sourced each customer.

## How to use a prompt

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chat">
    Open **Chat** in Lemonado, or open a connected AI tool via MCP (such as Claude or ChatGPT).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the prompt">
    Browse the full library at [lemonado.io/prompts](https://lemonado.io/prompts) and copy the prompt you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste and adjust">
    Paste the prompt into the chat. Adjust any placeholder values like `[client name]` or date ranges to match your context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your analysis">
    Lemonado queries your live data and returns the analysis. Every answer is grounded in real numbers from your connected accounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The best prompts are specific. If a prompt returns a broad answer, add context like "for [client name]" or "for campaigns running in the last 14 days" to narrow the results.
</Tip>
