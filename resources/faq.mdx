## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Lemonado?">
    Lemonado is an AI-native data platform for marketing agencies and in-house teams. It connects every tool you use into one live data layer, then gives you Chat, Studio, agents, and MCP connectors on top — so your team and your AI tools work from the same real-time context.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    Account creation takes about 2 minutes. Each integration takes around 30 seconds via OAuth. A typical full setup is complete in under 30 minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. You get a 10-day free trial of the AI-First plan. No credit card required. Sign up at [data.lemonado.io](https://data.lemonado.io).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I already use ChatGPT or Claude. Why do I need Lemonado?">
    ChatGPT and Claude can each connect to individual tools in isolation — but they have no shared definitions, no client context, and no governance. Ask a real cross-platform question across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, GA4, and revenue, and you get a different answer every time — or no answer at all.

    Lemonado is the layer underneath. It blends every platform into one governed, query-ready dataset, keeps persistent context per client and brand, and exposes that to your AI tools via a single MCP connection. The AI tool you already use stays — the answers are just grounded in your actual marketing data, scoped, read-only, and consistent across your whole team.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data & Integrations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What tools does Lemonado integrate with?">
    Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, Google Analytics, Google Search Console, YouTube, Stripe, Google Sheets, CSV uploads, Snowflake (Enterprise), ClickHouse (Enterprise), Sharetribe, WhatConverts, Instagram Organic, and Facebook Organic. More integrations are added regularly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often is my data updated?">
    Most ad platforms refresh in real time. Other sources refresh as frequently as their API allows. You can see the exact refresh cadence for each source in the integration settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can Lemonado modify my ad campaigns or budgets?">
    No. All data connectors are strictly read-only. Lemonado cannot create, edit, pause, or delete campaigns, ads, or budgets in any connected platform.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I need an integration that isn't supported yet?">
    Reach out to [hello@lemonado.io](mailto:hello@lemonado.io) — the team can prioritize new integrations or discuss a custom connection. In the meantime, upload your data via Google Sheets or CSV to keep working.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI Accuracy & Trust

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Lemonado ensure answers are accurate?">
    Every question triggers a real SQL query against your live data. Answers include full source attribution — you can see exactly which rows the answer came from and trace the logic end to end. Answers are never speculative.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I verify the numbers Lemonado gives me?">
    Yes. Every answer shows the source data behind it. You can trace any metric back to the raw query to confirm the numbers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data used to train AI models?">
    No. Your data is never used to train AI models.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security & Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data hosted?">
    Your data is hosted on Google Cloud in the EU. All connections are encrypted in transit and at rest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Lemonado GDPR compliant?">
    Yes. Data is hosted in the EU, all integrations are read-only, and Lemonado signs Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) on request. Contact [hello@lemonado.io](mailto:hello@lemonado.io) to request a DPA.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can see my data inside my organization?">
    You control it. Each workspace has its own member list and permissions. You can scope access to specific clients, channels, or data sources, and remove anyone at any time. The AI only ever sees what the person asking the question is permitted to see.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is client data isolated between clients?">
    Every client has its own workspace with its own connected data, members, and persistent memory. Queries, agents, and AI context are all scoped to a single workspace — one client's data is never mixed with another's, and the AI cannot reach across workspaces.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing & Plans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a credit?">
    Credits are consumed when AI does work for you. Simple queries — like looking up yesterday's ad spend — use 1 credit. Complex cross-platform analyses that pull from multiple sources, compare time periods, and generate a full Studio can use 30 or more. See the [Pricing page](/resources/pricing) for examples.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    AI features pause until your credits reset at the start of your next billing period. You can top up credits at **$50 per 500 credits** or upgrade your plan at any time to get more credits immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel at any time?">
    Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime and remain active until the end of the current billing period. There is no lock-in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer annual billing?">
    Yes. Annual plans come with a discount. Contact [hello@lemonado.io](mailto:hello@lemonado.io) to switch.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
