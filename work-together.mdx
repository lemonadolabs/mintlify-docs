Marketing work moves faster when the answer and the conversation stay together. Lemonado helps your team find past work, share a chat, and ask for feedback in one place.

## Keep work easy to find

- Use the magnifying glass to search earlier chats.
- Pin work your team returns to often.
- Use **Projects** to group chats around a campaign or goal.
- Filter **Recents** when you only want to see unread or active work.

## Bring in a teammate

1. Open the chat you want to share.
2. Click the people icon and add a workspace teammate.
3. Your teammate can accept the invitation under **Invites** or from the bell.
4. Use comments and `@mentions` when you need a clear review or decision.

## Manage the workspace

Open your profile, then choose **Workspace settings**. Owners and admins can invite members and review billing and usage.

In an agency workspace, you can limit a teammate to selected clients. They only see the client spaces they need.

<Tip>
  Keep one chat for each decision. A clear title makes it much easier to find later.
</Tip>
