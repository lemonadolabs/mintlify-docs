## Keep work easy to find

- Use the magnifying glass to search earlier chats.
- Open a chat's menu in **Recents** and choose **Pin chat** to keep it close.
- Choose **Add to project** from the same menu to group chats around a campaign or goal.
- Filter **Recents** when you only want to see unread or active work.

## Bring in a teammate

1. Open the chat you want to share.
2. Click the chat title to open **People**, then choose **Add** and select a workspace teammate.
3. Your teammate can accept the invitation under **Invites** or from the bell.
4. Mention a teammate when you need their feedback. Mention **@Lemonado** when you want the assistant to reply in a shared chat.

## Manage the workspace

Open your profile and choose **Settings**. Owners and admins can invite **Members** and view **Usage**. Only the workspace owner can open **Billing**.

In an agency workspace, you can limit a teammate to selected clients. They only see the client spaces they need.

<Tip>
  Keep one chat for each decision. A clear title makes it much easier to find later.
</Tip>

Prefer Slack, Teams, or email? [Set up a team channel](/team-channels) to ask Lemonado there.
