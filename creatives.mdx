## Find what matters

- Switch between ads, accounts, campaigns, and ad sets.
- Choose a date range.
- Pick the metrics your team uses.
- Filter and sort to surface winners or weak spots.
- Save a view you want to revisit.

Click an ad to see its details, comments, and insights.

## Find new ideas

If your workspace has **Discover**, use it to browse ads by brand, country, platform, or category. Save ideas to a board for your next creative review.
