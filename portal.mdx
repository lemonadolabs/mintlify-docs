The Portal gives a client a simple view of the work you choose to share. They do not need access to your full Lemonado workspace.

<Frame caption="Use Config to set the portal name, URL, logo, colors, and appearance.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/portal.jpg" alt="Client Portal configuration with branding and appearance settings" />
</Frame>

## Set up the portal

1. Open the client and choose **Portal**.
2. Open **Config** to review the portal name, look, and access.
3. Turn the portal on when it is ready.
4. Use **Open portal** to check the client view.

## Share work

- Publish approved reports or dashboards from **Studio**.
- Share a saved ad view from **Creatives**.
- Return to **Portal → Shared** to confirm what is visible.

<Warning>
  Review dates, filters, and client details before you share. Turning the portal off hides all shared content from the client.
</Warning>
