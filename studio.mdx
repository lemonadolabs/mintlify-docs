Studio is where Lemonado turns analysis into something your team or client can use.

<Frame caption="Open recent work, start from a template, or create something new.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/studio.jpg" alt="Lemonado Studio with completed dashboards and suggested next builds" />

## Create something new

1. Open **Studio** and click **New**.
2. Choose a report, dashboard, or deck.
3. Describe the audience and the decision the work should support.
4. Review the result and ask for changes in the attached chat.

You can also start from a template when you want a familiar structure.

<Frame caption="Keep the request and the live result together while you refine the work.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/studio-editor.jpg" alt="Studio dashboard editor beside the chat that created it" />
</Frame>

## Keep the result useful

- Give the work a clear name.
- Check the date range and source.
- Ask Lemonado to shorten, explain, or restyle a section.
- Share only after you have reviewed the result.
