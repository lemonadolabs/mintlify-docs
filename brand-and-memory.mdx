Tell Lemonado who you help, what you sell, and how you want to sound. It can use that context in future work.

Click the gear icon beside the company name.

## Brand

Review the logo, colors, fonts, description, and brand notes. Add writing guidance such as “Use short sentences and avoid industry jargon.”

## Memory

Review the company knowledge and instructions. Add missing details about your audience, offer, competitors, or goals. Correct anything that is out of date.

<Frame caption="Start with your website, add a note, or drop in a useful file.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/memory.png" alt="Memory settings with company website research and Add the first note options" />
</Frame>

## Other settings

- **Slack**, **Teams**, and **Email** let you [talk to Lemonado outside the app](/team-channels).
- **External access** lets [other AI apps read this company's data](/external-ai).
- **Pulse** lets you turn opportunities and suggestions for repeat work on or off.
- **Company settings** holds the company details.

<Tip>
  Only owners and admins can change these settings. Ask yours if you spot something that needs updating.
</Tip>
