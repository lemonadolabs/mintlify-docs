Good context helps Lemonado give answers that fit the company, not a generic marketing playbook.

Click the gear icon beside the company name.

## Brand

Add the company name, website, logo, colors, fonts, and writing style. Lemonado uses these details when it creates client-facing work.

## Memory

Review what Lemonado knows about the company, including its offer, audience, competitors, and goals. Add files or instructions when something is missing.

## Other settings

- **Slack** chooses where Lemonado can chat.
- **External access** controls which tools Lemonado can use.
- **Pulse** controls the opportunities shown on Home.

<Tip>
  Update Memory when the strategy, offer, or audience changes.
</Tip>
