**About Lemonado**

We're building the context layer for the agentic era. AI agents today have amnesia. They don't know your clients, their history, or their data. Lemonado fixes that. We unify data across every marketing and business tool into persistent, client-scoped workspaces where humans and AI agents collaborate with full context. One platform replacing the entire stack of reporting tools, dashboards, and data pipelines that agencies have been stitching together for years.

We're a small team in Stockholm. Paying customers. Real traction. And a massive market that's about to shift from dashboards to conversational, agent-driven intelligence. We want to be the platform that shift runs on.

**The role**

Don't see a role that fits but know you'd be great at Lemonado? We still want to hear from you. The right person often creates the right role, and we'd rather meet you now than miss you later.

This is for engineers, designers, go-to-market, operators, and anyone in between. If you're already obsessing about the AI-native shift in marketing, agencies, or data tooling, you're probably someone we want to talk to.

**How to apply**

- Tell us what you do and what you'd want to work on at Lemonado.
 
- Share a link to your work: LinkedIn, portfolio, GitHub, writing, anything that shows how you think.
 
- Tell us why Lemonado specifically. What about this problem, this category, or this team made you reach out.
 

We read every application and respond within a week.

**Why join Lemonado**

- Be part of a cutting-edge company shaping the future of marketing data in the AI era.
 
- Work in an AI-native environment with a small, ambitious team.
 
- Competitive base salary with significant equity upside.
 
- Work directly with the founders from day one.
 

**Details**

- Full-time, Remote.
 
- Competitive salary and meaningful equity.
 
- Work directly with the founders.
 

We interview on an ongoing basis. Please submit your application sooner rather than later. We're committed to building a diverse and inclusive team: if you're excited about working with us but don't see a role that fits perfectly, we encourage you to apply anyway.
