**About Lemonado**

Lemonado is the AI-native data and intelligence platform for marketing teams. We unify data across every marketing and business tool into one workspace, where humans and AI agents work together with full context. Marketing agencies and in-house teams use Lemonado to replace the patchwork of dashboards, reporting tools, and data pipelines they've been stitching together for years.

We're a small team in Stockholm. Paying customers. Real traction. And a massive market that's shifting from dashboards to conversational, agent-driven intelligence. Design is how we make that shift feel obvious.

**The role**

We're looking for a designer who cares deeply about craft. Someone early in their career but already obsessive about the details. You'll work directly with the founders on everything design touches: product, marketing site, brand, content.

Most of the job is making the product feel really great to use. The kind of small, considered details that make people love a tool and tell their friends about it. Studio is one surface where you'll get to do that, but it runs through everything we ship.

This isn't a role where you'll be handed Figma files to clean up. You'll own real projects end-to-end, from rough idea to shipped pixels. We'll mentor you, push you, and expect you to grow fast.

**What you'll work on**

- Design product features alongside the founders and engineering. Flows, prototypes, hi-fi visuals, the works.
 
- Sweat the product details. Microinteractions, empty states, loading, transitions, the small things that turn a good tool into one people love.
 
- Push our brand and marketing site forward. Landing pages, feature pages, illustrations, motion, social.
 
- Help shape and maintain our design system. Components, tokens, patterns that scale.
 
- Make Studio feel inevitable. Templates, theming, polish, the small details that turn a prompt into something a customer wants to send to their CEO.
 
- Work with AI tools as part of your daily workflow. Generate, iterate, refine.
 

**Who you are**

- You have a portfolio that shows taste. Maybe one job, maybe internships, maybe just side projects you couldn't stop polishing. We care about the work, not the years.
 
- Strong in Figma. Comfortable with prototyping, components, auto-layout.
 
- Visual + interaction sense. You can take a half-formed idea and turn it into something clear and beautiful.
 
- Curious and self-driven. You ship without being told exactly what to do.
 
- Excited by AI-native design. You use Claude, ChatGPT, Midjourney, and others as part of your workflow.
 
- Bonus: motion design chops. After Effects, Rive, Lottie, or whatever you reach for to bring an interface to life.
 
- Bonus: you know Framer well enough to push our marketing site directly.
 

**Why this matters**

The category we're building is brand new. The companies that win it are the ones with the best craft, not the loudest marketing. You'll help define how AI-native data tools should look and feel, from product to marketing to brand. Few startups give a junior this much surface area. We do.

**Details**

- Part-time, Stockholm.
 
- Competitive compensation.
 
- Work directly with the founders.
