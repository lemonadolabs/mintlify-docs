## Frequently Asked Questions

What tools does Lemonado integrate with?

Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, Pinterest Ads, Snapchat Ads, Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Google Sheets, Stripe, Snowflake, ClickHouse, and more. See the [full list on integrations](http://lemonado.io/../integrations).

Does Lemonado replace Looker Studio?

Yes. With Studio, you build dashboards and reports by chatting, on live data, with no manual data pipelines, no paid connectors per source, and no drag-and-drop widget building. Studio refreshes in real time, not on a cron.

How does Lemonado ensure AI answers are accurate and trustworthy?

Every question runs as a real SQL query against your live data, with full source attribution. You can see exactly which rows the answer came from, trace the logic, and verify the numbers. Answers are explainable, not speculative.

What is the unified data layer and why does it matter?

The unified data layer combines every tool you use into one connected, real-time dataset. Spreadsheets, APIs, ad platforms, Stripe, Google Ads, GA4, and more. It's the foundation that powers Chat, Studio, agents, and MCP connectors. No manual exports, no complex setup, no stale data.

How often is my data updated?

Most ad platforms refresh in real time. Other sources refresh as frequently as their API allows. You can see the exact refresh cadence per source in the integration list.

Can I white-label reports for my clients?

Yes. Studio supports your logo, brand colors, and custom domains so reports look like they came from you, not from Lemonado.

Can my clients log in to view their own data?

Yes. You can give clients view-only access to their workspace and the Studios you publish for them. They see only their own data, never another client's. Great for replacing weekly decks with a live, always-on dashboard.

Can I export Studio reports as PDF?

Yes. Any Studio can be exported to PDF for sharing offline. Most teams share the live link instead, since it stays up to date automatically.

Can I schedule Studio reports to send automatically?

Yes. Use a custom agent to refresh and deliver a Studio on any cadence (daily, weekly, monthly) to Slack, email, or in-app. Recipients always see the latest live numbers, not a stale snapshot.

How do I migrate from Looker Studio, Supermetrics, or Funnel.io?

Most teams run Lemonado in parallel for a couple of weeks, rebuild the dashboards they actually use (much faster than the original since you describe them in chat), then turn the old tools off. We'll help you do it. Send your existing dashboards or report templates to [hello@lemonado.io](mailto:hello@lemonado.io) and we'll match them in Lemonado for you.
