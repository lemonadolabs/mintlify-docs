A workspace is Lemonado's fundamental organizing unit. Every client or team you manage gets its own dedicated workspace — complete with connected data sources, persistent AI memory, and scoped access controls. Everything you do in Lemonado happens inside a workspace: asking questions, building reports, storing context, and managing data. Think of each workspace as a self-contained environment where the AI learns that account's full picture and every output stays scoped to the right client.

## Creating a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Clients in the left sidebar">
    Open **Clients** from the left navigation to see your full list of workspaces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the + icon">
    Click the **+** icon in the top right of the Clients panel to start creating a new workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the client name and website URL">
    Type the client's name and paste in their website URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lemonado scrapes basic company info automatically">
    Lemonado performs a quick web scrape of the URL and pre-fills basic company information into the workspace — so you don't start from a blank slate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lemonado auto-matches connected data accounts">
    Lemonado scans your connected data sources and automatically links any accounts whose names match the client name. Auto-matched accounts appear highlighted in green in the **Data** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's inside a workspace

## Adding context

Open the **Context** tab inside any workspace. You'll find the company information Lemonado scraped when you created the workspace — edit any section at any time. To add new context, click **Add Context** in the top right, create a folder with a title, and fill in whatever the AI needs to know: goals, KPIs, budget constraints, audience segments, reporting tone, campaign history, seasonal trends, or any account-specific rules and definitions.

Think of the Context tab as a persistent client brief that's always loaded into every chat and every report. The AI reads all of it every time you ask a question or generate output for that workspace. The more context you add, the sharper every response gets — good inputs equal good outputs.

## Linking data to a workspace

Open the **Data** tab inside the workspace. Accounts that Lemonado automatically matched to the client appear highlighted in green. Review these to confirm they're correct, then manually add any accounts that didn't match automatically — for example, if an ad account name doesn't match the client name exactly. Once all the right accounts are linked, every chat and report in that workspace draws only from that client's data. No filtering or extra setup required.

## Sharing and access

Invite teammates to a workspace directly from the workspace settings. Each member you add can access the workspace's chat, reports, and data based on the role you assign them.

You can also give clients access to their own workspace. Assign them **view-only** access so they can review reports and dashboards, or **contributor** access so they can ask questions and leave comments — without ever touching another client's workspace.

Role-based permissions enforce a hard boundary between workspaces. One client can never see another client's data, reports, or chat history. Access is scoped at the workspace level, and the AI only ever sees what the person asking the question is permitted to access.

## Persistent memory

Lemonado builds context over time. Every conversation, every report you generate, and every piece of context you add to the Context tab makes the AI sharper for that workspace. This memory persists across sessions and across team members — so a new colleague joining the workspace benefits from everything the team has already built, and you never have to re-explain the same account twice.

<Tip>
  For agencies, create one workspace per client. For in-house teams, you can create workspaces per brand, product line, or department.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Workspaces are isolated by design. Data, memory, and outputs in one workspace are never accessible from another.
</Note>
