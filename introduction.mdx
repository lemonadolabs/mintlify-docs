Lemonado is the AI-native marketing data platform built for agencies and in-house teams who are tired of fighting fragmented tools. Connect every platform you run — Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, GA4, Stripe, and more — and Lemonado blends them into a single, governed, real-time data layer your whole team can query in plain language, turn into branded reports, and automate with AI agents.

## What is Lemonado?

Most marketing teams operate in a state of chronic data fragmentation. Ad spend lives in one tab, analytics in another, revenue in a third — and every AI conversation starts from scratch because you're pasting stale CSV exports into ChatGPT.

Lemonado replaces that duct-taped stack with a **unified data layer**: a single, continuously-synced dataset that blends every platform you use into one query-ready source of truth. Every question you ask runs as a real SQL query against your live data — not a guess, not a cached snapshot. You see exactly which rows the answer came from, so insights are explainable and trustworthy.

The critical difference from pasting CSVs into ChatGPT is governance and context. Lemonado maintains persistent context per client and brand, enforces consistent metric definitions across platforms, and exposes your data to external AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, n8n) through a single scoped, read-only MCP connector — so the AI tool you already use gets real answers, not hallucinations.

## Who uses Lemonado?

**Marketing agencies** use Lemonado to manage their entire book of business from one platform. Instead of rebuilding context for each client meeting or exporting data to assemble reports by hand, agencies give every client a dedicated workspace with persistent memory — goals, KPIs, campaign history, and definitions all stored and instantly accessible. What used to take hours of report-wrangling now takes minutes, and clients receive branded, AI-written reports that arrive automatically on schedule.

**In-house marketing teams** use Lemonado to break down the silos between their ad platforms, analytics tools, and revenue data. Performance leads, growth managers, and even leadership can ask questions in plain language and get immediate, sourced answers — without waiting on an analyst or learning SQL. Proactive agents keep the whole team informed when something shifts, so teams spend their time on strategy rather than status updates.

## Security and data privacy

Lemonado is designed to meet modern privacy, security, and governance expectations without adding operational complexity:

- **Read-only connectors** — every integration connects via read-only access. Lemonado cannot create, edit, pause, or delete campaigns, ads, or budgets in any connected platform.
- **Client-scoped workspaces** — data and outputs are isolated by workspace so only permitted users and AI agents can access a given client's data.
- **EU data hosting** — all data is stored in the EU, giving you a clear, predictable data residency story.
- **GDPR compliant** — Lemonado is GDPR compliant and signs Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with customers on request. Email [hello@lemonado.io](mailto:hello@lemonado.io) to request your DPA.
- **Never used to train AI** — your data is never used to train AI models. What you connect stays yours.

## How it works

**1. Connect** — Go to the **Data** tab and connect your ad platforms, analytics tools, and revenue sources via OAuth or API key. Most integrations take around 30 seconds and begin syncing immediately. Data from ad platforms refreshes in real time; other sources update according to their own API schedules.

**2. Chat / Build** — Open **Chat** and ask any question in plain language — Lemonado translates it into a live SQL query and returns a sourced answer with full data attribution. When you're ready to build something more structured, switch to **Studio** and prompt your way to a branded dashboard, report, or presentation — no drag-and-drop required.

**3. Automate** — Turn any recurring workflow into an automated process. Schedule reports to deliver to clients via email or Slack on a fixed cadence. Set up proactive **Agents** to monitor your KPIs and alert you when something changes. Create an **MCP Connector** to give Claude or ChatGPT persistent, governed access to your live marketing data — so every AI interaction starts with real context, not a blank slate.
