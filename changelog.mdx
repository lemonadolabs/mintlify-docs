  ## PDF exports and easier everyday workflows

  Download your [Studio](/studio) reports and slides as PDFs, ready to send to a client or bring to a meeting. Open your work and choose **Share → Export as PDF**.

  - **Find your work faster.** Filter Studio by dashboards, reports, or slides, and combine the filters with search.
  - **Review comments alongside your work.** Studio comments now sit beside the preview, so you can follow the discussion while reviewing it.
  - **Start with a routine template.** Browse the available templates in [Routines](/routines) without installing them in your workspace first. Choose one and tailor it to the job you want to repeat.
</Update>
