Connectors bring information from the tools you already use into Lemonado.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    Choose **Connectors** in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your tool">
    Search by name or browse the featured connections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it">
    Select the tool and follow the sign-in steps. Choose the account you want Lemonado to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    Open the **Connected** tab. Your source appears here when it is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Search for a tool, then follow its connection steps.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/connectors.jpg" alt="Connectors page with search, featured marketing tools, and a connected source" />

<Tip>
  Begin with one high-value source. Add more when you need a wider view.
</Tip>

You can also upload a CSV. If your tool is missing, ask your workspace admin for help connecting it.
