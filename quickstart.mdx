---

By the end of this guide you'll have a live data source connected, a real question answered with your actual marketing data, a client workspace set up, and your first report generated — all inside Lemonado. Most steps take under a minute each.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [data.lemonado.io](https://data.lemonado.io) and sign up for a free account. Every new account starts with a **10-day free trial of the AI-First plan** — no credit card required. You get full access to every feature so you can evaluate Lemonado with your real data before committing to a plan.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect a data source">
    1. Click **Data** in the left sidebar.
    2. Click **Connect source** in the top right.
    3. Click the icon for the integration you want to add. If you're running paid ads, **Meta Ads**, **Google Ads**, or **Google Analytics** are good starting points.
    4. Authenticate via OAuth — the whole flow takes around **30 seconds**.
    5. Select the ad accounts or properties you want to include.

    Your data source now appears in your data library. Data refreshes automatically — most ad platforms update in real time, and you can always come back to add or remove accounts or adjust parameters.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Click **Chat** in the left sidebar. You'll see a familiar interface — just like ChatGPT or Claude. Type any question in plain language and Lemonado will run a live query against your connected data and return an answer with the numbers to back it up.

    Try one of these to get started:

    ```
    "What was my ROAS last week broken down by campaign?"
    "Which ad sets spent over $1,000 with no conversions this month?"
    "Compare CPA across Meta and Google for the last 30 days."
    ```

    Every answer includes full source attribution so you can trace exactly where the numbers came from.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a client workspace">
    1. Click **Clients** in the left sidebar.
    2. Click the **+** icon to create a new client.
    3. Enter the client's **name** and **website URL**.

    Lemonado scrapes basic company information from the website and automatically identifies which of your connected accounts belong to this client based on the account names. Each client gets a dedicated workspace — with its own chat, data, dashboards, reports, and context — so nothing bleeds between accounts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate a report">
    1. Open the client workspace you just created.
    2. Go to the **Reports** tab and click **New Report** in the top right. Alternatively, open the **Chat** tab and ask for a report directly — Lemonado will open the report builder automatically.
    3. Describe what you want. For example:

    ```
    "Build a weekly Meta Ads report with spend, ROAS, CPA, and CTR.
    Include a bar chart by campaign."
    ```

    The AI builds the report step-by-step in real time. Once the draft is ready, make manual edits like you would in a Google Doc, apply a brand theme, and save. Share it via a direct URL or schedule it for automatic email or Slack delivery.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up an AI connector (optional)">
    If you want to use your Lemonado data inside Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or n8n, set up an **AI Connector** using MCP (Model Context Protocol).

    1. Click **AI Connectors** in the left sidebar.
    2. Click **New AI Connector**.
    3. Follow the setup guide for your preferred AI tool.

    Each connector is scoped to a specific client or your whole workspace, and is always read-only. See [AI Connectors overview](/ai-connectors/overview) for detailed setup instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Need help at any point? Email the Lemonado team at [hello@lemonado.io](mailto:hello@lemonado.io) — they're happy to help you get set up.
</Note>
