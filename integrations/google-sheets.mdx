The Google Sheets integration lets you bring spreadsheet data into Lemonado. Use it for targets, budget trackers, custom KPI benchmarks, or any data that lives in a spreadsheet but belongs alongside your marketing analysis. Lemonado fetches the latest version of your sheet in real time — no manual re-uploads required.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data sources">
    Go to **Data** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Connect source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Google Sheets">
    Select **Google Sheets** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Complete the OAuth flow — authorize Lemonado to read your Google Drive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your sheet">
    Select the specific sheet or sheets you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start syncing">
    Data syncs whenever the sheet is updated. Your sheet data is now available in Chat and Studio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example use cases

- Connect a budget tracker sheet and ask: _"Which campaigns are pacing over budget vs. my planned spend in the sheet?"_
- Connect a monthly KPI targets sheet and ask: _"Are we on track to hit our MQL target based on current Google Ads performance?"_
- Connect a commission tracking sheet and ask: _"Reconcile this month's Stripe revenue with the commission calculations in my sheet."_

<Note>
  Google Sheets data is read-only. Lemonado cannot create, edit, or delete any sheets, cells, or files in your Google Drive.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Google Sheets is also a great way to bring in data from integrations Lemonado doesn't yet support natively. Export from the source tool, paste into a Sheet, connect it to Lemonado, and it becomes queryable alongside all your other data sources.
</Tip>
