GA4 traffic, conversion events, user behavior, and performance metrics — all queryable alongside your ad platform and revenue data in Lemonado. Authentication is handled via OAuth, and data syncs in real time the moment you connect.

## What data is available

- Sessions, users, pageviews, bounce rate, and session duration
- Conversion events and goals
- Traffic source and channel breakdowns
- Device, geography, and demographic reports
- E-commerce event data (if configured in GA4)
- All data is read-only

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data sources">
    Go to **Data** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Connect source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Google Analytics">
    Select **Google Analytics** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Complete the OAuth flow — you'll be redirected to Google to authorize access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your GA4 property">
    Select the GA4 property you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start syncing">
    Data starts syncing immediately. Your GA4 data is now available in Chat and Studio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example queries

Once connected, ask questions like these directly in Chat:

- "Which traffic source drove the most conversions last week?"
- "Show me daily organic sessions vs. paid sessions for the last 30 days."
- "What is the mobile vs. desktop conversion rate breakdown this month?"
- "Which landing pages have the highest bounce rate among paid traffic?"
- "Compare conversion rates across countries for Q2."

## Cross-channel analysis

With GA4 connected alongside your ad platforms, you can run attribution analysis directly in Chat. For example, ask: _"How much Google Ads spend led to Stripe revenue this quarter?"_ Lemonado joins the data across sources automatically — no manual exports or spreadsheet merges required.

<Note>
  Google Analytics data is read-only. Lemonado cannot create, edit, or delete any GA4 properties, events, or configuration.
</Note>
