The TikTok Ads integration brings your video ad performance data into Lemonado, covering campaigns, Ad Groups, and individual ads. Query video-specific metrics — view rate, play actions, completion rate — alongside spend and conversion data from all your other ad platforms, in a single chat.

## What data is available

- Campaign, Ad Group, and Ad performance metrics (impressions, clicks, conversions, spend, CPM, CPC, CTR, CVR)
- Video performance metrics (views, view rate, video play actions)
- Creative-level data
- All data is read-only

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data in the sidebar">
    Go to **Data** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Connect source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select TikTok Ads">
    Select **TikTok Ads** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Complete the OAuth flow to grant Lemonado read access to your TikTok Ads account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your ad accounts">
    Select the ad accounts you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start querying">
    Data starts syncing. Head to Chat to run your first query.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example queries

Ask Lemonado questions like these once your TikTok Ads account is connected:

- "Which TikTok creatives have the best video completion rate this week?"
- "Compare cost per click on TikTok vs. Meta for our awareness campaigns."
- "Show me TikTok ad sets with high spend but low conversion rate."

<Note>
  TikTok Ads data is read-only. Lemonado cannot create, edit, pause, or delete campaigns, ads, or budgets.
</Note>
