CSV upload lets you bring any tabular data into Lemonado without a live integration. It's ideal for one-off imports, data from platforms that don't have a direct connector yet, or quick exports you want to cross-reference with your live marketing data.

## How to upload a CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data sources">
    Go to **Data** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Connect source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select CSV">
    Select **CSV** from the list of available options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Upload your CSV file from your local machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the schema">
    Lemonado parses the schema automatically — review the detected column names and data types and confirm they look correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start querying">
    The dataset is now available for querying in Chat and Studio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for CSV uploads

Follow these guidelines to get the best results from Lemonado's automatic schema detection:

- **Include a header row** with clear, descriptive column names — Lemonado uses these to understand the structure of your data
- **Use a consistent date format** — `YYYY-MM-DD` works best and avoids parsing ambiguity
- **Keep numeric columns clean** — remove currency symbols and commas (use `1000`, not `$1,000`)
- **For recurring datasets**, consider using Google Sheets instead so your data stays up to date automatically without re-uploading

## Example use cases

- Upload a CSV export from an unsupported ad platform and compare its performance to Meta and Google Ads data
- Import a customer list for segmentation analysis alongside your Stripe data
- Upload offline conversion data and cross-reference it with online ad spend to measure true campaign ROI

<Tip>
  For data that updates regularly, use the <a href="/integrations/google-sheets">Google Sheets integration</a> instead of CSV. Connect a sheet once and Lemonado picks up changes automatically — no re-uploading needed.
</Tip>
