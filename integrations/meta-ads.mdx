The Meta Ads integration brings your Facebook and Instagram ad performance data into Lemonado, covering the full ad hierarchy — campaigns, ad sets, and ads. Query spend, creative results, audience breakdowns, and conversion events alongside your Google, LinkedIn, TikTok, and other connected channels. Authentication is via OAuth and data syncs in real time.

## What data is available

- Campaign, ad set, and ad performance metrics (impressions, reach, clicks, conversions, spend, CPM, CPC, CTR, ROAS)
- Creative-level data (ad previews, images, video thumbnails)
- Audience breakdown by age, gender, placement, and device
- Conversion events from the Meta Pixel
- All data is read-only

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data in the sidebar">
    Go to **Data** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Connect source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Meta Ads">
    Select **Meta Ads** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Complete the OAuth flow — you'll be redirected to Facebook to authorize access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your ad accounts">
    Select the ad accounts you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start querying">
    Data starts syncing immediately. Head to Chat to run your first query.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example queries

Ask Lemonado questions like these once your Meta Ads account is connected:

- "What was my Meta Ads ROAS last week by campaign?"
- "Which ad sets have zero spend in the last 48 hours?"
- "Flag any Meta Ads with overspend risk compared to daily budget."
- "Show me CTR trends for my top 5 creatives over the last 14 days."
- "Compare CPA on Facebook vs. Instagram placements this month."

## Creative analytics

Meta Ads data powers Lemonado's [Creative Analytics](/features/creative-analytics) feature. Once you connect your Meta Ads account, Lemonado can surface fatigue scores and hook scores across your active creatives — so you can act before performance drops.

<Note>
  Meta Ads data is read-only. Lemonado cannot create, edit, pause, or delete campaigns, ads, or budgets.
</Note>
