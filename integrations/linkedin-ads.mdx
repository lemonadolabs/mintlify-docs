The LinkedIn Ads integration brings your B2B paid campaign data into Lemonado, covering Campaign Groups, campaigns, and individual ads. Query CPL, lead gen form submissions, and audience segment performance alongside your Google, Meta, and TikTok data — all in a single chat or report.

## What data is available

- Campaign, ad set (Campaign Group), and ad performance metrics (impressions, clicks, conversions, spend, CPL, CPC, CTR)
- Lead gen form submissions and conversion tracking
- Audience segment performance
- All data is read-only

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data in the sidebar">
    Go to **Data** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Connect source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select LinkedIn Ads">
    Select **LinkedIn Ads** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Complete the OAuth flow to grant Lemonado read access to your LinkedIn Ads account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your ad accounts">
    Select the ad accounts you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start querying">
    Data starts syncing. Head to Chat to run your first query.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example queries

Ask Lemonado questions like these once your LinkedIn Ads account is connected:

- "Which LinkedIn campaigns generated the most leads last month?"
- "Compare CPL across LinkedIn, Google, and Meta for this quarter."
- "Identify LinkedIn audiences with the highest conversion rate."
- "How much did we spend on LinkedIn Ads with zero conversions last week?"

<Note>
  LinkedIn Ads data is read-only. Lemonado cannot create, edit, pause, or delete campaigns, ads, or budgets.
</Note>
