The Stripe integration brings payment and revenue data — customers, subscriptions, invoices, and transactions — into Lemonado. Query it alongside your ad platforms to close the loop between marketing spend and actual revenue, without exporting a single spreadsheet.

## What data is available

- Customer records and revenue by customer
- Subscriptions, MRR, ARR, and churn
- Invoices and payment events
- Transaction history
- All data is read-only

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data sources">
    Go to **Data** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Connect source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Stripe">
    Select **Stripe** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Complete the OAuth flow — authorize Lemonado to read your Stripe data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start syncing">
    Data starts syncing immediately. Your Stripe data is now available in Chat and Studio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example queries

Once connected, ask questions like these directly in Chat:

- "What is MRR and churn rate this month vs. last month?"
- "Which marketing channels drove the highest revenue customers?"
- "Find customers who started a free trial but never converted to paid."
- "What percentage of this month's Stripe revenue came from Google Ads leads?"
- "Show me customers by lifetime value, segmented by acquisition channel."

## Revenue attribution

Connect Stripe alongside Google Analytics and your ad platforms to run full-funnel attribution analysis. Ask questions like _"Which campaign drove the most LTV-positive customers last quarter?"_ — Lemonado joins ad spend, behavioral data, and Stripe revenue across sources to answer it directly.

<Note>
  Stripe data is read-only. Lemonado cannot create or modify charges, subscriptions, or customers.
</Note>
