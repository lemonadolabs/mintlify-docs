Google Search Console provides organic search performance data — queries, impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position. Connect it to Lemonado to analyze SEO performance alongside paid media in one place, so you can stop switching between tools to compare organic and paid results.

## What data is available

- Search queries and keywords driving organic clicks
- Impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position
- Page-level performance breakdowns
- Device and country segmentation
- All data is read-only

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data sources">
    Go to **Data** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Connect source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Google Search Console">
    Select **Google Search Console** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Complete the OAuth flow — authorize access in Google when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your property">
    Select the property (domain) you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start syncing">
    Data starts syncing. Your Search Console data is now available in Chat and Studio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example queries

Once connected, ask questions like these directly in Chat:

- "Which search queries drove the most clicks last week?"
- "Show me keywords where we rank between positions 4 and 10 — opportunities to move to page 1."
- "Compare organic CTR for our top 10 landing pages this month."
- "Are we spending on branded keywords where we already rank #1 organically?"

<Note>
  Search Console data is read-only. Lemonado cannot modify your Search Console properties, settings, or verified sites.
</Note>
