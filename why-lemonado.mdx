Use Lemonado when you need an answer from your marketing data, a report to share, or a regular check you do not want to repeat by hand.

## For marketing teams

Ask one question across your connected tools. Follow up on the result without copying numbers into another spreadsheet.

For example: compare last month's campaigns, investigate a rise in cost per lead, then create a short report for your next meeting.

## For agencies

Give each client a separate space for their data, brand, chats, and reports. Reuse the same reporting template or review process across clients.

Set up [Routines](/routines) for recurring checks and use the [client portal](/portal) to share work you have reviewed.
