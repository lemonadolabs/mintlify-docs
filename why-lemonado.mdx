Marketing work often lives across ad platforms, analytics tools, spreadsheets, chats, and slide decks. The hard part is not finding more data. It is turning that data into a clear next step.

Lemonado gives your team one place to ask, create, and keep watch.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Ask" icon="message-circle">
    Get a clear answer from your connected sources.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create" icon="palette">
    Turn the answer into a report, dashboard, or deck.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep watch" icon="clock">
    Schedule checks and hear about changes that matter.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## For marketing teams

You spend less time collecting numbers and more time deciding what to do. Lemonado keeps your brand, goals, and past work close to every conversation.

## For agencies

Each client gets a separate space for their data, brand, work, and shared results. Your team can reuse proven skills and templates without mixing client information.
