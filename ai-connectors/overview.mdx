Lemonado AI Connectors use the [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) to expose your governed marketing data to external AI tools. Instead of pasting CSVs or exporting reports, your AI tool queries your live data directly through a secure, read-only connection. Every query runs against Lemonado's unified SQL layer — combining ad platforms, analytics tools, and business data in a single request.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your data sources to Lemonado">
    Add your platforms — Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, Stripe, and more — from the **Integrations** page in Lemonado.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open AI Connectors">
    Go to **AI Connectors** in the Lemonado sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new connector">
    Click **New AI Connector** and select the workspace you want to expose to your AI tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your authentication method">
    Select **OAuth** (recommended) or **Bearer token** authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your credentials">
    Copy the MCP server URL and any authentication credentials generated for your connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste into your AI tool">
    Open your AI tool's connector settings and paste in the MCP server URL and credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start querying">
    Ask questions in Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP-compatible tool — your AI tool now queries your live marketing and revenue data directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## MCP server details

Use the following server URL when adding Lemonado to any MCP-compatible tool:

```
MCP Server URL: https://mcp.lemonado.io/mcp
```

**Supported authentication methods:**

- **OAuth** (recommended) — secure delegated access with token refresh
- **Bearer token** — static token for tools or automation workflows that don't support OAuth

## Security and governance

Every Lemonado AI Connector is built around a read-only, scoped, and auditable access model.

- **Read-only** — AI tools can query and analyze your data, but can never modify or delete it. Your underlying systems are protected at all times.
- **Per-workspace scoping** — each connection is limited to a single workspace, so your AI tool only sees the data you explicitly choose to expose.
- **Revocable** — revoke any connection instantly from the **AI Connectors** settings page in Lemonado, without touching the AI tool itself.
- **Logged** — every query made through an AI Connector is recorded and auditable inside Lemonado.
- **No credential exposure** — your underlying platform credentials (Google Ads tokens, Meta access tokens, etc.) are never shared with the connected AI tool. The AI sees only the governed data layer Lemonado provides.

## Credits

Queries made through MCP connections consume credits from your Lemonado account, the same as in-app chat queries. Simple lookups — like fetching yesterday's spend for one campaign — use fewer credits than complex cross-platform analysis that joins data from multiple sources.
