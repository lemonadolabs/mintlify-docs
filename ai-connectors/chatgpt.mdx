This guide walks you through adding Lemonado as an MCP connector in ChatGPT. Once connected, ChatGPT can query your live marketing data — Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, Stripe, and more — directly in the chat interface, with no exports, no CSVs, and no copy-pasting required.

## Before you begin

<Note>
  Make sure you have the following before starting:

  - An active Lemonado account with at least one data source connected (10-day free trial available at [data.lemonado.io](https://data.lemonado.io/signup))
  - A **ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Education** plan — custom MCP connectors require one of these plans
</Note>

## Step 1: Enable custom connectors in ChatGPT

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to ChatGPT">
    Navigate to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your **profile icon** in the bottom-left corner, then select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Advanced Settings">
    Go to **Apps → Advanced Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Developer Mode">
    Toggle on **Developer Mode** to unlock custom MCP connector support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Custom MCP connectors are available on ChatGPT web for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans.
</Note>

## Step 2: Add Lemonado

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new app">
    In **Settings → Apps**, click **Create app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your connector details">
    Fill in the following:

    - **Name:** `Lemonado`
    - **MCP Server URL:** `https://mcp.lemonado.io/mcp`
    - **Authentication:** OAuth (recommended)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the connector">
    Click **Create** to save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize ChatGPT in Lemonado">
    ChatGPT redirects you to Lemonado. Log in and authorize access to your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    After authentication completes, Lemonado appears as a connected tool in your ChatGPT settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  ChatGPT may display a warning about custom MCP servers accessing external data. Lemonado is fully read-only — it can query and analyze your data but cannot modify or delete anything in your connected platforms.
</Note>

## Step 3: Activate Lemonado in a chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new chat">
    Open a new chat in ChatGPT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the tools menu">
    Click the **Tools / Apps / Connectors** button near the message input box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Lemonado">
    Choose **Lemonado** from your list of connected tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it is active">
    Verify that Lemonado is shown as enabled for the current chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once active, ChatGPT automatically routes relevant data questions to Lemonado, querying your live marketing data in real time.

## Example queries

With Lemonado active in the chat, try questions like:

- "Compare cost per purchase by channel over the last 30 days."
- "Show blended CAC across all ad platforms for Q1."
- "Which campaigns have high spend but no conversions in the last 14 days?"
- "Which campaigns across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn drove the highest ROAS this week?"

ChatGPT queries Lemonado's unified SQL engine to answer these questions — joining data from multiple platforms in a single response, all in real time.

## Managing the connection

To revoke or remove the connector, go to **Settings → Apps & Connectors**, select **Lemonado**, and choose to revoke access or remove the connector entirely.

You can also rotate OAuth credentials at any time directly inside your Lemonado workspace under **AI Connectors** — no action required on the ChatGPT side.

## Best practices

- **Scope access appropriately** — only connect the data sources that are relevant to the queries you plan to run.
- **Monitor query logs regularly** — review the Lemonado query log to track how ChatGPT is accessing your data and catch any unexpected usage.
- **Rotate OAuth tokens periodically** — refresh tokens on a regular schedule and remove any connectors that are no longer needed.
