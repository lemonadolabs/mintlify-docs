This guide walks you through adding Lemonado as an MCP connector inside Claude. Once connected, Claude can query your live marketing data — Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, Stripe, and more — directly in chat, without you pasting a single CSV or exporting anything.

## Before you begin

<Note>
  Make sure you have the following before starting:

  - An active Lemonado account (a 10-day free trial is available at [data.lemonado.io](https://data.lemonado.io/signup))
  - At least one data source connected inside Lemonado
  - A [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) account (any plan)
</Note>

## Step 1: Open connector settings in Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Claude">
    Navigate to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your **profile icon** in the top-right corner, then select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Connectors">
    In the Settings sidebar, click **Connectors**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom connector">
    Click **Add custom connector** to open the connector configuration window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can create multiple Lemonado connectors inside Claude — one per client, team, or use case — each pointing to a different Lemonado workspace.
</Note>

## Step 2: Add Lemonado as a connector

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your connector details">
    In the **Add custom connector** window, fill in the following:

    - **Name:** `Lemonado`
    - **MCP Server URL:** `https://mcp.lemonado.io/mcp`
    - **Authentication:** OAuth (recommended)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the connector">
    Click **Add** to save the connector configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin authentication">
    Click **Connect** to start the OAuth flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Claude in Lemonado">
    A browser window will open. Log into Lemonado and authorize Claude to access your workspace data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Once authentication completes, the connector shows as **Connected** in your Claude settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Claude may display a warning that this connector is built by an external developer. Lemonado is fully read-only — it can query and analyze your data but cannot modify or delete anything in your connected platforms.
</Note>

## Step 3: Activate Lemonado in a chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new chat">
    Open a new chat in Claude.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the connector picker">
    Click the **+** icon in the chat input bar to open the files, connectors, and tools menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Lemonado">
    Scroll through the list of available connectors and select **Lemonado**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle it on">
    Enable Lemonado for the current chat. Claude will now automatically route relevant data queries to your Lemonado workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Lemonado inside Claude Projects

If you use [Claude Projects](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/9517075-what-are-projects) to organize work by client or team, enable Lemonado inside the Project to keep the connection active across every chat in that Project.

When Lemonado is enabled at the Project level, Claude can combine:

- **Project context** — strategy docs, brand guidelines, reporting templates
- **Conversation history** — prior decisions and notes accumulated in the Project
- **Live marketing data** — real-time campaign metrics queried directly from Lemonado

For agencies, this creates a persistent AI analyst per client — structured data, historical context, and strategic documentation all in one place, always current.

## Example queries

Once Lemonado is active in a chat, try questions like:

- "Which campaigns across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn drove the highest ROAS this week?"
- "Compare cost per purchase by channel over the last 30 days."
- "Which campaigns have spent over $5,000 with no conversions this month?"
- "Break down revenue by campaign and ad set for last quarter."

Claude queries Lemonado's unified SQL engine to answer these questions in real time — combining data from multiple platforms in a single response.

## Managing the connection

To disconnect or remove the connector, go to **Settings → Connectors** in Claude, select **Lemonado**, and choose **Disconnect** or **Remove**.

You can also revoke OAuth access directly from your Lemonado workspace under **AI Connectors** — this immediately invalidates the token without requiring any action inside Claude.

## Best practices

- **Scope access appropriately** — only connect the data sources your team needs for the queries you plan to run.
- **Review query logs periodically** — monitor the Lemonado query log to track how Claude is accessing your data.
- **Rotate tokens regularly** — refresh OAuth tokens periodically and remove any connectors that are no longer in active use.
