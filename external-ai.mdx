Already use another AI app? **External access** lets it read the data connected to a company in Lemonado.

You need to be a workspace owner or admin to set this up.

## Connect an app

1. Open the company and click the gear beside its name.
2. Choose **External access** and turn the connection on.
3. Select your AI app and follow the instructions shown in Lemonado.
4. Ask a question in that app to check the connection.

Lemonado calls this connection an **MCP**. You do not need to write code to follow the in-app setup.

<Warning>
  Only connect apps you trust with the company's data. In an agency workspace, check that you have opened the right client first.
</Warning>

To stop access, return to **External access** and turn the connection off.
