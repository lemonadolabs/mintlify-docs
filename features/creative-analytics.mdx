Creative Analytics is a dedicated view inside Lemonado for analyzing ad creative performance across every major ad platform. Connect your accounts and get fatigue scores, hook scores, spend breakdowns, and pivot tables — all in one place. Because Creative Analytics is part of Lemonado's unified data layer, insights flow directly into Chat and Studio, so a finding never stays stuck in a chart. Spot a fatiguing ad and follow up in Chat, or surface your top performers in a Studio report without switching tools or exporting anything.

## Supported platforms

Creative Analytics works across the platforms where your ad spend lives:

- Meta Ads
- Google Ads
- LinkedIn Ads
- TikTok Ads
- YouTube

## Key metrics

Creative Analytics tracks the signals that tell you what's working and what needs attention.

- **Fatigue score** — tracks when a creative's performance starts declining due to audience saturation. Catch issues before they drain budget, not after the damage is done.
- **Hook score** — measures how effectively an ad captures attention in the first few seconds. A low hook score is the first place to look when CTR underperforms.
- **CTR trends** — week-over-week click-through rate trends per creative. See at a glance whether a creative is gaining or losing traction.
- **CPC trends** — cost-per-click over time, segmented by ad set. Rising CPC on a static creative is a reliable early fatigue signal.
- **Spend breakdown** — shows exactly where spend is going across platforms and creatives. Stop guessing which creatives are consuming budget.

## Pivot tables

Slice and group your creative data by platform, campaign, time period, audience, and more. Results update as you click — no export required. Filter, pivot, and slice millions of rows in seconds. Use pivot tables to answer questions like "which creative is spending the most on LinkedIn versus Meta?" or "how does hook score compare across campaigns this month?" without writing a single query.

## Taking action

Creative Analytics connects to the rest of your Lemonado workflow so insight leads to action:

- **Follow up in Chat** — spot a fatiguing ad, click into it, and ask follow-up questions directly in Chat to dig into the root cause.
- **Share with clients or the team** — generate a signed share link for any winning ad and send it to a client or teammate without granting full account access.
- **Comment in context** — leave comments on specific ads so creatives and strategists can discuss performance in the same place they're looking at the numbers.
- **Surface top performers in Studio** — automatically pull top-performing creatives into a Studio report so clients always see what's winning.

## Shareable lists

Create and save structured lists that organize your creative data into reusable views: top performers by ROAS, top fatigue alerts, regional filters, customer review highlights, and more. Share these lists directly with clients or the creative team so everyone is working from the same curated set of ads — not digging through the full account.

## Common questions to answer with Creative Analytics

Use Creative Analytics to get fast, accurate answers to the questions that drive creative decisions:

- "Which creative has the best CTR on Meta this week?"
- "Which ads are showing fatigue signals and should be refreshed?"
- "Compare hook scores across our top 5 TikTok ads this month."
- "Where is spend going on creatives that haven't converted in 14 days?"
