Lemonado Chat is a collaborative AI chat interface that runs real SQL queries against all of your connected marketing data — Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, GA4, TikTok, Stripe, and more — and returns answers grounded in live numbers with full source attribution. Stop bouncing between platforms to piece together a picture. Ask one question and get one answer, instantly, from every source at once.

## What you can ask

Chat handles the full range of marketing questions you'd normally spend hours chasing across tabs. Here are some examples organized by type.

**Cross-platform performance**

Compare results across every channel without leaving a single thread:

- "What was my blended ROAS across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn last week?"
- "Which channel had the lowest CPL this month?"

**Spend analysis**

Spot waste and reallocate budget with confidence:

- "Which ad sets spent over $1,000 with no conversions?"
- "Where am I overspending for low-quality leads?"

**Creative performance**

Identify what's working before you scale it:

- "Which creative has the best hook rate on Meta this week?"

**Revenue attribution**

Connect ad spend directly to pipeline and revenue:

- "What percentage of Stripe revenue came from Google Ads leads?"

## How answers work

Every question you ask triggers a real SQL query against your unified data layer. Lemonado doesn't summarize or estimate — it runs the query, returns the result, and shows you exactly where the numbers came from. You can see which rows the answer is based on and trace the full logic step by step. Answers are always explainable, never speculative.

<Note>
  Chat answers always reflect your live, connected data. If a data source hasn't been connected yet, Lemonado will tell you rather than guess.
</Note>

## Collaborative chat

Chat is built for teams, not just individuals. Bring your whole team into the same conversation and work on the data together in real time.

- **@mention teammates** to invite them into any chat session — they join with full context already loaded
- **See live cursors** — everyone in the session sees the same conversation and the same data at the same time
- **Leave comments** within conversations to flag insights, ask follow-ups, or hand off threads to a colleague
- **Share prompt libraries** across the team so everyone starts from the same high-quality questions

## Chat in Slack

Install the Lemonado Slack app to bring Chat into any channel without switching tools. Each Slack connection is scoped to a specific workspace — a client account, an internal team, or your whole company — so the right data is always in scope. Ask questions directly in Slack and get answers powered by the same live, unified data as the full Lemonado interface. No copy-pasting, no context switching.

## Attaching files

Drop a brief, screenshot, or deck directly into a Chat session. Lemonado reads the attached document alongside your live data and gives you context-aware answers that bridge what's in the file with what's in your connected platforms. Brief says CPA target is $40? Lemonado will tell you which campaigns are hitting it and which aren't.

## Client access

Invite a client into a specific workspace with view-only or contributor access. They can ask their own questions against that workspace's data, see the same live numbers you see, and follow the conversation in real time. They never see any other client's workspace, and you control their permission level at all times. Use this to replace weekly reporting calls with always-on, self-serve access.
