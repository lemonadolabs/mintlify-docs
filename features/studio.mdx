Studio is the visual layer in Lemonado where you build dashboards, reports, multi-page documents, and presentations — all powered by your live, connected marketing data. Describe what you want in plain text and Studio constructs it in real time, replacing tools like Looker Studio and Supermetrics without the drag-and-drop complexity, paid connectors, or stale syncs.

## Building with Studio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reports tab">
    Navigate to the **Reports** tab inside any client workspace. Alternatively, ask in Chat — the AI will open the report builder automatically when it detects you want to build something visual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Report">
    Click **New Report** to start with a blank canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    Type what you need in plain language. For example:

    > "Build a weekly Meta Ads report with spend, ROAS, CPA, and CTR. Include a bar chart by campaign and a line chart of spend over time."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it build in real time">
    Lemonado constructs the report step-by-step in front of you — you watch each section appear as it's built, populated with live data from your connected sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit by chatting">
    Click any element on the canvas and type what you want changed. Chat with individual widgets or the entire report at once:

    - "Make this a bar chart."
    - "Group by week."
    - "Restyle the whole report in dark mode."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and theme">
    Save the report and apply a theme to match your client's brand or your agency's colors. Every saved report stays live — the data updates automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can build

Studio handles any reporting format your team or clients need:

- Weekly and monthly client performance reports
- Quarterly board presentations
- Live marketing dashboards
- Multi-page campaign summaries
- Embeddable spend and revenue overviews

## Data sources in Studio

Studio blends any connected source onto a single canvas. Connect Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, GA4, TikTok, Stripe, and more — Studio pulls them all together automatically, with no manual joins or data pipeline setup required. Apply a filter and every widget on the canvas updates in milliseconds. Data is always live, not a snapshot pulled on a schedule.

## Automated delivery

Schedule any saved report to deliver itself on your chosen cadence without any manual work.

1. Go to the **Reports** tab in a workspace and click **Schedule** on any saved report
2. Set the frequency (daily, weekly, or monthly), time zone, and exact delivery time
3. Add the email addresses or Slack channels that should receive each delivery
4. Each reporting cycle automatically adjusts metrics and commentary for the period being reported — no manual date-range edits needed

## Sharing and embedding

Get reports in front of stakeholders wherever they already are:

- **Share a signed link** — anyone with the link sees the live, up-to-date report in their browser, no login required
- **Embed anywhere** — use signed embed URLs to drop a live report into Notion, an intranet, a client portal, or any webpage
- **In-context comments** — stakeholders can comment directly on the report page, not over email
- **Export to PDF** — download a static PDF snapshot when you need to share offline or attach to an email

## White-labeling

If you run an agency, Studio works under your brand. Add your logo, apply your brand colors, and set a custom domain so every report your clients receive looks like it came from your agency — not from Lemonado. Build once, deliver everywhere, under your name.

## Studio vs. Looker Studio

| | Looker Studio | Lemonado Studio |
|---|---|---|
| Build method | Drag-and-drop widgets | Describe in chat |
| Data freshness | Scheduled syncs | Real-time |
| Multi-source joins | Requires setup | Automatic |
| Collaboration | Google account required | Multiplayer, any user |
| White-label | Not available | Logo, colors, custom domain |
| Embed URLs | Signed embed URLs | Signed embed URLs |
| Cost | Per seat + connector fees | Single subscription |

<Tip>
  To migrate from Looker Studio or Supermetrics, run Lemonado in parallel for a week or two and rebuild only the dashboards you actually use — it's much faster the second time since you describe them in chat rather than drag-and-drop. Send your existing dashboards to [hello@lemonado.io](mailto:hello@lemonado.io) and the team will match them in Lemonado for you.
</Tip>
