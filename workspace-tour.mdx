## Find your way around

- Click **New chat** when you want a clean start.
- Use **Recents** to return to earlier work.
- Click the gear beside the company name for **Memory**, **Brand**, and company settings.

## Stay organized

- Use the magnifying glass to search earlier chats.
- Open the bell for notifications and invitations.
- Open your profile at the bottom left, then choose **Settings** for personal and workspace settings.
- Workspace owners can manage members, billing, and usage there.

<Accordion title="If you see Files or Warehouse">
  These areas appear only when enabled for your workspace.

  - **Files:** choose **Personal** for your own files or **Workspace** to share with the team. Use **Upload** to add files and **New folder** to organize them.
  - **Warehouse:** explore your data in tables. Ask the assistant to filter or explain a view, then **Save** it for later. Check who the saved view is available to.
</Accordion>
