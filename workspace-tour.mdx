| Area | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Ask a question or start a piece of work. |
| **Creatives** | Review ads, compare results, and find inspiration. |
| **Studio** | Create reports, dashboards, and decks. |
| **Connectors** | Add the tools and data Lemonado can use. |
| **Skills** | Use ready-made ways of completing common marketing jobs. |
| **Tasks** | Schedule work and monitor what needs attention. |
| **Portal** | Share approved work with a client. |

## Three useful shortcuts

- Click **New chat** when you want a clean start.
- Use **Recents** to return to earlier work.
- Click the gear icon to manage company memory, brand, Slack, and access.

## Stay organized

- Use the magnifying glass to search earlier chats.
- Open the bell to see work that needs your attention.
- Open your profile at the bottom left for personal and workspace settings.
- Workspace owners can manage members, billing, and usage in **Workspace settings**.

  Start from **Home** when you are unsure where to go. Describe the outcome you want, and Lemonado can help you choose the next step.
