Agency work moves quickly, but each client still needs the right data, brand, and context. Lemonado gives every client a separate space while keeping your team in one workspace.

## Set up your agency

Choose **Agency** when you create the workspace. Add one client at a time with the company name and website.

Use the client switcher at the top of the left menu to move between clients or return to the agency overview.

## Use the agency overview

The overview holds the tools shared across your agency:

- **Home** shows your clients and work that needs attention.
- **Shared connectors** manages sources your agency uses across clients.
- **Ad syncs** shows whether client ad data is arriving.
- **Templates** keeps reusable report, dashboard, and deck layouts.
- **Skills** keeps the workflows your team can reuse.

Click the gear icon to update your agency brand and instructions.

For each client:

1. Connect their marketing sources.
2. Review their Brand and Memory.
3. Create reports and dashboards in Studio.
4. Schedule the work your team repeats.
5. Share approved results through their Portal.
